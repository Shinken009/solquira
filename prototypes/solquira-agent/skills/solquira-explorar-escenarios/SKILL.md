---
name: solquira-explorar-escenarios
description: Explore how water availability and crop history change Solquira demonstration alternatives, without certifying agricultural viability.
---
Read leer_escenario to understand the selected synthetic scenario. Ask about missing inputs rather than inventing them. Available profiles are valle, secano and precordillera; water is an integer from 40 to 120 in steps of ten; history categories are cereal, leguminosa, cubierta and descanso.

Call comparar_escenarios to run the engine. Omitted changes preserve the selected inputs. Only propose changes when requested, and preserve the returned comparison_id and revision. Never equate rainfall with irrigation supply or a score with eligibility. An exploratory comparison does not save or replace the user's original scenario.
