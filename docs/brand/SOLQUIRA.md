# Solquira — Understand the land. Cultivate the future.

Public brand confirmed by the owner on 27 October 2026. Local implementation and review candidate; not a new public deployment.

## Positioning & Social Mission

**Understand the land. Cultivate the future.**

### Why Solquira?

Solquira unites two root concepts: **Sol** (the sun that beats down on warming lands) and **Quia** (the Latin word for _"because"_ or _"the reason why"_).

Under accelerating climate change, rising temperatures and intense solar radiation threaten the survival of smallholder farming. But land vulnerability is not just an ecological statistic—it is a human crisis. When soil degrades, families lose their livelihoods, local economies fracture, and rural communities are displaced by desertification.

Meaningful environmental intelligence must do more than document decline. It starts with asking **why** the land is suffering—and stands alongside growers to decide **what to do next**.

### Our Mission

Born from the frontlines of drought and desertification in the semi-arid valleys of northern Chile, Solquira is a place-aware land-care workspace built with growers, advisers and communities—not above them.

When the model path is enabled, Solquira uses **NVIDIA Nemotron through Nebius Token Factory** to improve three short action details. Python owns the soil and water bands, action order, labour labels and pathway catalogue. When inference is unavailable, field rules keep the brief complete in the same card.

We believe climate adaptation starts with better questions, verifiable evidence and practical choices that keep soil alive and support local livelihoods. The product supports decisions; it does not promise agronomic, financial or environmental outcomes.

## Visual direction — living field atlas

The mark is **sun over two contour lines**. Read literally it is a valley in perspective; read as a figure, it is a person standing in that landscape — the people who care for the land. The sun is the warm accent; the lower contour carries the watercourse teal, so the three brand colours map to sun, land and water held in the soil. It replaced the watershed-Q because at 40 px the ring, arcs and stem read as a magnifier or a wifi symbol. The mark does not force the initial: the wordmark carries the name, the symbol carries the place. The same native SVG geometry serves the header, purpose band, About, footer and favicon. Trademark clearance remains a separate release consideration.

The layout now distinguishes three jobs instead of repeating one kind of card:

1. **A place to begin:** an open-paper, landscape-led introduction and a clear location register.
2. **A field instrument:** the brief holds three coloured readings — soil, water and weather — with dark, readable labels. On a phone the two soil readings share a row and the outlook spans the full width.
3. **A next visit:** a tactile checklist, crop-path notes and a self-contained execution receipt.

The corrected valley-and-river illustration remains contextual rather than masquerading as satellite evidence. It carries the social focus through the grower and cultivated landscape, not through invented impact statistics or testimonials.

## Modes and copy

The public/static variant says **Sample workspace**. Its main journey is a regional map, crop-path examples and a downloadable land-care record. It does not advertise a forecast button that cannot run. The connected variant keeps the forecast → question → field-brief journey.

Repeated warnings about saved explanations, absent inference and unassessed viability are removed from primary copy. “Methods & sources” retains the execution basis, synthetic assumptions and field-suitability limitations. Provider failures, stale data and session recovery remain explicit at the point of failure.

Calculations, source validation, financial limits and scientific output fields are unchanged. A display label is not evidence of an activated integration.
